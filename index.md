## Current Readings 

Here are a few latest books that I am reading/just finished. I am happy to receive book recommendations from anyone!
- **Remains of the Day (Kazuo Ishiguru):** A butler obsessed with his work reflects back in time serving for Lord Darlington during 1920s and 30s. The narrative style is eerily peculiar and quite interesting in my opinion. I actually liked this book of Ishiguro more than Never Let Me Go.
- **Origins of Political Order (Francis Fukuyama):** This is one of the quintessential non-fictions I have read. A deep historical analysis of political institutions: tribalism, chiefdom, role of ideas, religion etc. What impressed me most about the book is the author's cross civilizational analysis of developmental history of states in China, India, Europe and the Islamic world, which is extremely difficult to put together in one cover. This book *educated* me on history in a profound way. 
- **A Brief History of Intelligence (Max Bennet):** I had heard a lot of good reviews of this book from people, and the book is indeed *that* good. What I found most interesting from the book is the big leaps in neuroscience that explains primate intelligence and how it connects to modern AI. I, being ignorant of neuroscience literature, found all the neuroscience development -- specially how hypotheses related to mouse brains are tested with controlled experiments -- to be absolutely fascinating. This book greatly updated my mental model of the nature of animal intelligence.
